# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients and business owners — primarily non-technical or semi-technical — landing on this page to evaluate whether Daniel Wissa is worth hiring for automation, AI, or engineering work. They are not browsing; they arrived with a problem and are deciding whether Dan can solve it profitably. Secondary: technical peers and recruiters who assess credibility through depth and specificity of work shown.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio landing page that converts visitors into contacts. The page's job is not to list credentials — it is to make a potential client feel that working with Dan is an obvious, profitable decision. Success means the visitor reads through, understands the value delivered, and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Creative, technical, ambitious. The page should feel like it was built by someone who takes their craft seriously — not a freelancer hustling for gigs, but a sharp engineer with a clear point of view. Tone: direct, confident, zero fluff. Reference energy: Stripe — polished, trustworthy, conversion-aware.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark-sidebar dev portfolio templates (the standard GitHub Pages aesthetic)
- Corporate HR-page feel — stiff, impersonal, interchangeable
- Cheap freelancer site — cluttered, low-signal, no authority
- Gimmick-first design — animations as the main event, substance buried

## Design Principles

1. **Value before vanity.** Every section answers the client's implicit question: "What's in it for me?" Lead with outcomes, not tools or titles.
2. **Show, don't tell.** The use cases and experience sections carry the persuasion — concrete specifics beat adjectives.
3. **Expert confidence.** No hedging copy, no filler sections. Design choices are decisive. If something is on the page, it earns its place.
4. **Conversion clarity.** The page has one job: get the contact. Layout, hierarchy, and CTAs all funnel toward that outcome.
5. **Craft as signal.** The quality of the page itself is proof of capability. A poorly built portfolio undermines every claim inside it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Sufficient color contrast on all text. No information conveyed by color alone.
